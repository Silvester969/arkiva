What Arkiva is

Arkiva keeps a family's papers in one private place on the iPhone: ID cards and passports, bills, bank letters, contracts, medical records, school certificates. Scan them with the camera, or add them from Photos and Files, and find any of them later by any word in them. The app is in Albanian, with English available in Settings, and is made for Albanian documents.

  • Encrypted on the phone. Every document is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is saved. The key lives in the iPhone's Secure Enclave and opens only with Face ID or the device passcode.
  • Read on the phone. Text is recognised on the device with Apple's text recognition. Search ignores ë and ç, understands Albanian word endings and forgives small typos.
  • Organised. Multi-page documents, family members tagged automatically, categories, and a trash that keeps deleted documents for 30 days.
  • Recoverable. A 24-word recovery phrase on paper, and an encrypted backup file you can restore on a new phone.
  • For the whole family. Family phones join by scanning a QR code on a phone that is already in the family.
  • Nothing collected. No accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The developer never receives your documents. Free.
Keep the 24 words safe. If the recovery phrase is lost and no family phone can open the archive any more, nobody can open the documents again, not even the developer.

Çfarë është Arkiva

Arkiva mban letrat e familjes në një vend të sigurt në iPhone: letërnjoftime, pasaporta, fatura, kontrata, dokumente banke dhe shëndetësie. Skano me kamerën ose shto nga Fotot dhe Skedarët, dhe gjej çdo dokument me çdo fjalë që përmban. Çdo dokument enkriptohet në telefon dhe hapet vetëm me Face ID ose me kodin e telefonit. Pa llogari, pa reklama, pa gjurmim, falas.
